Kids care health guide

Health Tips Watch

A healthy life for kids needs good food, plenty of play, and regular sleep. Children thrive when they eat fresh fruits and vegetables, drink water instead of sugary drinks, run and play for an hour each day, and get a full night of rest.

Good Food and Drink

  • Eat fruits and veggies of many colors.
  • Drink water when thirsty.
  • Start with breakfast every day.
