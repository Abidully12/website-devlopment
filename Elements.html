JoJo's Bizarre Adventure

JoJo's Bizarre Adventure(often called JJBA) is a popular Japanese manga and anime series written and illustrated by Hirohiko Araki It follows the long story of the Joestar family across many different time periods.

The story is split into nine parts, with each part having a new main hero who has the nickname "JoJo". The family fights evil forces like vampires, ancient beings, and other powerful fighters.
